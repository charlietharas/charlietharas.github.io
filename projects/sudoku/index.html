Sudoku

Threw together a quick Sudoku generator and solver after learning about graph coloring in Algorithms. Note:

  • All generated boards contain between 31-41 digits.
  • All generated boards have a unique solution.
  • Boards are generated by randomly populating the 3x3 submatrices on the diagonal. Thus, only ~1M (9! * 3) possible random boards can be generated, from which many more random solutions are generated by random digit removal.
