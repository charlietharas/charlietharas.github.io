Black Mirror Index

Though the greed and reckless abandon of those in Silicon Valley is ever-present as nauseating background anxiety, with the hopelessness of our situation made clear whenever the soulless eyes of Peter Thiel or Sam Altman flash by on our social media feeds, it is difficult to quantify.

So, mostly as a joke to amuse myself, I "created" this "index" to demonstrate just how estranged today's business leaders are from reality. Black Mirror is an athology series that depicts how technology can be abused and act parasitically off of our worst instincts. Episode quality and reception varies, but even the better ones tend to feel rather on the nose. Well, unless you're raising venture capital. Then apparently this stuff is business advice.

This is by no means comprehensive, scientific, or professional in any way. Also as a disclaimer, many episodes function as social commentary on existing technology, while others posit movie magic that will never be feasible--I have scored these regardless. If I'm missing something, let me know at nerd@charliemax.dev. Stay safe and put down the phone!
